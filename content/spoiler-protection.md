# Spoiler protection

Spoiler controls hide episode information; they are not parental controls or a filter for mature content.

## Hide unwatched episode information

1. Select your Plozz profile.
2. Open **Settings > Screens & Navigation > All screens**.
3. Enable **Hide spoilers for unwatched episodes** on Apple TV, or **Protect unwatched episodes** on iPhone/iPad.
4. Choose **Blur Thumbnail** or **Placeholder Art**.

Blur keeps the real thumbnail but obscures it. Placeholder Art uses series artwork instead of loading the episode image.

Protection treats episode images and text differently:

- An episode you have not started has its thumbnail hidden.
- An in-progress episode can show its thumbnail, but its title and overview stay hidden until watched.
- Watched episodes are not hidden.
- Movies, show artwork, and season artwork are not treated as unwatched episode thumbnails.

The result depends on the watched state supplied to Plozz. If information is hidden unexpectedly, check the active server user and episode history before marking anything watched.

The [Chapters](/playback/#jump-to-a-chapter) panel follows the same episode text and image rules.

## Hide scores separately

**Hide ratings until watched** works independently of episode spoiler protection. Both protections are off by default. It hides external ratings for unfinished movies, episodes, shows, and seasons, including in-progress items. Press or tap a blurred rating to reveal it.

To hide scores entirely in a header, use the review-score controls in [Home and detail-page settings](/home-and-navigation/) instead.

## Change Continue Watching cards

Open **Settings > Screens & Navigation > Home > Rows > Continue Watching**:

- **Artwork + logo:** wide movie or series artwork, not an episode still.
- **Thumbnail:** episode stills or movie artwork; episode images follow spoiler masking.
- **Poster:** upright movie or show posters, never episode thumbnails.

**Playback details** is separate: **Detailed** shows the play icon, progress, and playback information; **Progress only** keeps the progress bar without playback text. **Labels** independently controls text below the artwork: **Follow Appearance**, **On**, or **Off**. Hiding labels does not hide detailed playback information.

All three choices show artwork. **Continue Watching settings** remains available under Rows when the row is absent; use **Add row** to restore it. These profile presentation choices do not change watched state or row membership.

To remove an abandoned title from the row instead, use [Remove from Continue Watching](/library/).

## Choose what selecting a card does

In the same Continue Watching editor, set **Center button action** on Apple TV, or **When tapping an item** on iPhone/iPad. **Play** is the default; **Open detail page** lets you inspect a Home title before starting it. This does not change artwork or history.

On Apple TV, the remote's **Play/Pause** button still plays the focused item directly.
