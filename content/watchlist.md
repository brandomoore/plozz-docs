# Save and browse your Watchlist

Watchlist brings saved movies and shows together for the active **Plozz profile**. An entry does not grant playback access or request missing media.

## Save or remove a title

1. Select the intended Plozz profile and [server user](/profiles-and-locks/#choose-a-server-user).
2. Open a movie/show's details or hold its card, then choose **Add to Watchlist**.
3. Open **Watchlist** from navigation. On compact mobile layouts, check **More**. If hidden, [show its shortcut](/home-and-navigation/#arrange-shortcuts-and-details).
4. Choose **Remove from Watchlist** to undo a save.

On a show's details, the Watchlist action saves the show, not the highlighted episode.

**Removing…** marks a pending removal. A local confirmation does not prove every connected service has accepted the change; unavailable services can need another attempt.

## Filter and sort the page

On Apple TV, iPhone, and iPad, use the controls above the Watchlist grid:

- **Filter**: open **All** or the active filter, then choose **All**, **Movies**, or **TV Shows**. **Clear Filters** restores all titles without deleting saves.
- To sort, open the current label, initially **Date Added**. Under **Sort By**, choose **Date Added**, **Release Date**, or **Name**; change **Order** if needed.

Date Added defaults to newest first and uses Watchlist save dates, not library-import dates. Entries without a usable save date follow dated entries.

These browse choices belong to this profile and can [sync or transfer](/profiles-and-sync/). They arrange the Watchlist page, not its Home row.

## Understand connected lists

Watchlist combines explicit Plozz saves with enabled servers' and [trackers'](/trackers/) lists. Jellyfin/Emby contribute Favorites; Plex and Silo use their Watchlists. WeTrakr contributes its planning list.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Additions and removals can update connected lists too. Confirmed removals in a connected list can propagate to other lists. Check the viewer's server and tracker accounts before changing shared-account lists.

Disabling a server retracts its contributed entries, not explicit Plozz saves. **Don't use this server's watchlist** excludes that list without disabling browsing/playback; [server-user selection](/profiles-and-locks/#choose-a-server-user) explains how to reconsider.

For missing entries, clear filters and check the profile, server identity, enabled sources, and tracker connection. For an entry without a playable copy, check [library access](/library/#a-title-or-library-is-missing) or [Seerr requests](/media-requests/).
