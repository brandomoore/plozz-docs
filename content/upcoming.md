# Follow upcoming releases

Keep track of new episodes from shows you watch and digital releases for movies you have saved or requested. **Upcoming** is your calendar; [Discover](/discover/) offers broader suggestions.

## Open your calendar

Choose **Upcoming** in navigation. If hidden, open **Settings > Screens & Navigation > App navigation > Hide or Reorder Navigation**, hold **Upcoming**, then choose **Show**. On iPhone/iPad, check **More** if shown, or swipe the scrolling bottom shortcuts sideways.

Home's **Coming Soon** row previews upcoming titles. Open its **See all** card on Apple TV, or tap its heading's chevron on iPhone/iPad, to reach the calendar.

## Choose a date

Open the **Calendar** button and select a day. On a wide iPad layout, the calendar appears beside the titles; **Hide calendar** and **Show calendar** collapse or restore it.

The calendar covers the past 30 days through 90 days ahead. Open a card for the title's details. An announced date does not mean a playable copy has reached your library.

Movie dates are digital releases for your device's region, not cinema dates or guaranteed streaming-subscription availability. Episodes released together can share one card.

## Choose what appears

Open **Filters**, then work through these choices:

- **Type**: choose **All**, **Shows**, or **Movies**.
- **Interests**: keep **Watching** for episodes from shows with viewing history, **Watchlist** for saved movies, or **Requested** for active movie requests by this profile's [Seerr user](/media-requests/#connect-seerr). This is not the household's full request list.
- **Season premieres only**: keep first-episode releases without removing movies. **Hide watched movies** removes completed movies.

**Reset filters** restores all types and interests and turns both switches off. These calendar filters do not change Coming Soon.

## Hide or restore a title

Hold a calendar card and choose **Hide title** to remove that movie or show's releases from both Upcoming and Coming Soon. **Undo hide** reverses the latest hide; **Filters > Restore hidden titles** restores all hidden titles.

Hidden titles are saved for this profile on this device. Hiding does not remove a Watchlist entry or change your progress.

## If dates are missing

In **Filters**, choose **Refresh**; use **Retry** after **Some sources couldn't refresh.** Existing dates can remain while a source is unavailable.

For **No announced releases**, reset filters, check hidden titles and the active profile/server user, then confirm you have watched shows or saved movies. Dates depend on matching and announced schedules. Seerr is needed only for requests. On iPhone/iPad, turn **Offline Mode** off to refresh.
