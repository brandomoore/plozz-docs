# Trailers and previews

Preview a movie or show before watching, or keep Home and detail pages quiet while browsing. These choices belong to your Plozz profile.

## Watch a trailer

On a title's details, **Trailer** plays a server file in Plozz. Plozz prefers server trailers, including one from another accessible copy, before offering **Watch on YouTube**. That opens outside Plozz, independently of your external-player preference.

Apple TV needs the YouTube app; iPhone/iPad can fall back to its website. For **Couldn't open YouTube**, check that app. YouTube controls availability and restrictions. A trailer button appears only when a trailer is found; a visible link does not guarantee playback.

## Open a preview full screen on Apple TV

While a detail-page preview is playing, focus its action buttons and press Up to watch it full screen with sound and playback controls. The **Up for playback controls** hint identifies this action.

From Home's hero, Up does this only with **Pinned Sidebar**; other layouts keep their usual Up navigation. Press Back to return, closing any open menus or controls first. The preview's previous mute state is restored.

## Choose trailers and previews

Open **Settings > Screens & Navigation > Movie & show details > Trailers & music**. With the Home hero enabled, **Settings > Screens & Navigation > Home > Hero > Trailers & music** opens the same settings.

1. Under **Trailers**, choose **Off**, **Server only**, or **Server + YouTube**. Off removes trailer actions and autoplay, not other extras or theme music. YouTube links never autoplay.
2. Under **Home**, set **Home autoplay**, then **Start muted** if autoplay is on.
3. Under **Details**, choose **Detail background**: **Off** for still artwork, **Trailer** for an available server preview, or **Theme music**. Trailer has its own **Start muted**; theme music has [volume and preview controls](/audio/#control-detail-page-music).

Home and detail choices are independent. If trailers are Off, Home autoplay is unavailable and Detail background cannot use Trailer.
