# Video framing and upscaling

Fit the picture to your screen, reduce black borders, or enhance lower-resolution video. These choices do not change your streaming-quality limit.

## Choose how the picture fits

Reveal player controls. On Apple TV, open **More > Zoom Mode**. On iPhone/iPad, choose **Zoom** inside **Playback options** in portrait or **Playback settings** in landscape; both menus use an ellipsis.

- **Normal** fits the whole frame without stretching.
- **Auto-crop black bars** enlarges the detected picture proportionally. Some bars can remain; without detection, it fits the whole frame.
- **Crop** fills the screen by cutting off edges; **Stretch** changes proportions.
- **Custom** sets zoom from 50%-200%, relative to Normal.

Detection needs supported local playback, not AirPlay or Picture in Picture. If edge details or burned-in captions disappear, choose **Normal**.

On iPhone/iPad, Auto-crop initially reaches the screen edges, where a cutout can obscure the picture. Pinch inward to fit inside the safe area; pinch outward to restore edge fitting. That preference applies across this profile's Auto-crop videos and channels for this device type. Outside Auto-crop, pinching switches Normal/Crop instead.

## Set a starting choice

Open **Settings > Playback > Default zoom**. iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV have separate defaults, initially **Normal**. Choose **Auto-crop black bars** if you want automatic border reduction; it is optional. **Custom** reveals **Zoom**.

Your default applies to new video playback, including Live TV. Without Auto-crop detection, the picture fits normally. Disc menus keep Normal.

Choosing a mode or custom amount in a movie or episode remembers it for that movie or series, overriding the default. Matching copies across servers can reuse it. Live channel changes return to the default instead of remembering each channel's choice.

## Enhance lower-resolution video

Open **Settings > Playback > Video upscaling** for this profile:

- **Off** disables enhancement.
- **Automatic**, the default, selects Animation for anime or animation metadata, otherwise Standard.
- **Standard** enhances supported 8-bit SDR below 1080p.
- **Animation** enhances drawn SDR up to 1080p.

Choose Standard or Animation yourself if the metadata leads to the wrong choice.

Enhancement needs AetherEngine, supported hardware, and a larger display. HDR and 4K are not enhanced. It does not apply to Apple's native player or another player app. Choose Off to compare the original appearance.

Settings apply to new playback, including Live TV. During eligible seekable video, **More > Upscaling** on Apple TV or **Upscaling** in **Playback options**/**Playback settings** on mobile changes only the current session. Playback reloads around your position; this is not an instant comparison. Live TV, disc menus, separate-audio videos, and some share connections do not offer that switch.

If **Couldn't change upscaling** appears, choose **OK**, then try again or reopen the video.
