# Choose posters and artwork

Keep your library's chosen images, try online alternatives, or change just one part of Plozz. These settings change pictures, not your videos or progress.

## Choose the main source

1. Select the Plozz profile you want to change.
2. Open **Settings > Appearance > Artwork**.

Choose a preset:

- **Recommended** lets Plozz choose for each area.
- **Prefer my library's artwork** tries library images first, with online fallback.
- **Prefer artwork from metadata providers** tries online images first, with library fallback. Extra lookups can make images slower to appear.

Selecting a preset replaces this profile's individual artwork choices. It does not edit server images. Shared [Metadata Providers settings](/metadata-and-ratings/#choose-information-sources) still determine which online sources Plozz can use.

## Change one part of the app

Choose **Customize by view** to open **Artwork by view**. Select a row to cycle choices on Apple TV; on iPhone/iPad, choose from its menu.

Each row offers **Library** or **Metadata providers**. **Title detail pages** also offers **Mixed**: online backgrounds and logos, with library posters for related titles.

The groups follow this order:

- **Home**: **Showcase / hero** is separate from **Other Home rows**, which includes Home's Watchlist row.
- **Libraries**: **Browse** changes titles inside collections and playlists too. **Collections** and **Playlists** change their covers, not the titles inside.
- **Across the app**: **Watchlist page** is independent of Home. **Episode browser** changes thumbnails on show details, not **Video player artwork**.

**Top Shelf** appears only on Apple TV; **Downloads** appears only on iPhone/iPad. These choices affect pictures, not [spoiler protection](/spoiler-protection/) or playback.

## Posters in another language

**Settings > Language** also sets the preferred language for online TMDB and TheTVDB posters. When unavailable, neutral or other fallback images can appear. Library artwork stays as supplied; choosing a language does not translate it.

## Missing download images

Existing downloads keep valid saved images after artwork or language changes.

If a downloaded image is missing, turn [**Offline Mode**](/downloads/#browse-only-your-downloads) off and reconnect to the source. Use the profile and server user that downloaded it, and leave Plozz open. [Download network rules](/downloads/#choose-quality-and-networks) still apply.

Plozz retries missing images without downloading the video again. A missing poster alone does not mean the saved video failed; check **Downloaded** and [test playback offline](/downloads/#save-a-video).
