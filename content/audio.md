# Audio tracks and output

Choose a track in Plozz, then check your equipment's actual output. See [Plozz formats](https://plozz.app/formats) for supported formats.

## Select a language or track

Reveal playback controls and open **Audio** on Apple TV, or the **Audio** submenu in iPhone/iPad playback options.

For future sessions, set **Preferred audio language** in **Settings > Playback > Audio** on Apple TV, or Playback's **Tracks** section on iPhone/iPad. Choose original, device, or a language, separately for movies, TV shows, and anime.

**Remember audio language per series** remembers the language, not a particular track or mix. It can follow a matched series across servers. To choose a different commentary or surround track in the same language, select it in the player; series memory does not preserve that exact track.

For a wrong language, check series memory and the language preference. Neither can supply missing tracks.

Alternate versions can contain different languages or mixes; [change versions](/playback/) if needed.

## Improve dialogue on Apple TV

In the player's **Audio** menu, **Reduce Loud Sounds** and **Enhance Dialogue** appear when tvOS reports support for the current output, for video and Live TV. Enhance Dialogue can offer **Enhance More**, **Enhance**, and **Off**, or a toggle on outputs without levels.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> These change Apple TV-wide settings, not this Plozz profile. Their audible effect depends on the content and playback route; availability does not guarantee processing on every route.

If a change fails, try Apple TV Settings instead.

## Correct timing

The player has no adjustable audio delay. [Subtitle delay](/subtitles/#set-automatic-choices) is separate.

For lip-sync problems, compare another track, title, and output route. For buffering or dropouts, use [playback troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/), not subtitle timing.

## Keep audio playing on iPhone or iPad

Enable **Settings > Playback > Background Audio** to continue video audio when locking the device or leaving Plozz. It is off by default. This does not download the video: streaming can keep using data. AirPlay and Picture in Picture have [separate requirements](/platform-controls/#airplay-and-picture-in-picture).

## Control detail-page music

On Apple TV, iPhone, or iPad, open **Settings > Screens & Navigation > Detail Page > Background** and choose **Theme music**.

Adjust **Theme music volume** from 0% to 100%. Changing it can start a preview; use **Play preview** or **Stop preview** to compare levels. Leaving the page stops the preview. If no title theme is found, the preview uses a generated sample, not proof that your titles have music.

Available title music fades in while browsing details and pauses outside the app. It does not play alongside trailers or full video playback. Choose **Off** for static detail artwork without music or trailers; Home trailers and video audio tracks are independent.
