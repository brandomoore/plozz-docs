# Audio tracks and output

Choose your language, improve dialogue, match your equipment, or [identify a song](/playback/#identify-a-song-in-a-movie-or-show).

## Select a language or track

Open **Audio** in the player controls. In portrait on iPhone/iPad, use the ellipsis **Playback options > Audio** menu.

Set **Preferred audio language** in **Settings > Playback > Audio** on Apple TV, or Playback's **Tracks** section on iPhone/iPad. Choose a language, or enable **Set separately for movies, TV shows & anime** to use different preferences.

Plozz remembers a movie's manually chosen audio language. **Remember audio language per series** does the same for shows. Matching titles can reuse the language across servers, not an exact commentary track or mix.

Remembered choices override your general preference. Choose again in **Audio**, or try [another version](/playback/) for missing tracks.

## Choose output compatibility

In **Settings > Playback**, find **Prefer lossless audio** under **Audio**. It defaults on. Turn it off to request compressed surround-compatible output, particularly with regular HDMI ARC.

This affects AetherEngine video and Live TV, not Apple's native player or another app. Start the video or channel again after changing it. Output depends on the source and equipment.

It is shared by this device's Plozz profiles, not synced or transferred.

## Improve dialogue on Apple TV

The player's **Audio** menu offers **Reduce Loud Sounds** and **Enhance Dialogue** for video/Live TV when tvOS reports output support. Dialogue choices can be **Enhance More**, **Enhance**, and **Off**, or a toggle.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> These change Apple TV-wide settings, not this profile. Audible effects depend on content and playback route; availability does not guarantee processing.

If changes fail, use Apple TV Settings.

## Correct timing

If speech does not match lip movements, adjust audio timing during supported AetherEngine playback:

1. On Apple TV, open **Audio**, then its **Audio Sync** clock button. On iPhone/iPad, choose **Audio > Audio Sync…**; portrait places Audio inside **Playback options**.
2. Adjust **Audio Delay**. Positive values play sound later; negative values play it earlier. Use small changes and compare a speaking scene.
3. Release the control and wait to hear the result before closing the panel.

Choose **Reset** to undo your adjustment. Movies and episodes allow up to ten seconds either way; supported Live TV allows two. Changes can briefly skip or repeat audio, seek, or reload playback. The offset resets with a new stream.

The control is absent in Apple's native player, AirPlay's remote playback path, and streams that cannot seek or adjust audio timing. Live TV needs a usable rewind window. If the control is unavailable, try another audio track or output device. [Subtitle delay](/subtitles/#set-automatic-choices) changes captions only.

## Keep audio playing on iPhone or iPad

Enable **Settings > Playback > Background Audio** (default off) to listen while locked or outside Plozz. This does not download video; streaming can keep using data. AirPlay/Picture in Picture have [separate requirements](/platform-controls/#airplay-and-picture-in-picture).

## Control detail-page music

Open **Settings > Screens & Navigation > Movie & show details > Trailers & music**. Under **Details**, set **Detail background** to **Theme music**.

Set **Theme music volume** (0%-100%). Changes can preview automatically; use **Play preview** or **Stop preview**. Leaving stops it. Without a title theme, preview uses a generated sample, not your library's music.

Available themes fade in on details and stop when you leave or start playback. Choose **Off** there for still artwork. [**Home autoplay**](/trailers-and-previews/#choose-trailers-and-previews) and video tracks are independent.

On iPhone/iPad, use the detail page's speaker button (**Mute theme music**/**Unmute theme music**) while its theme plays. Muting lasts that detail session; it does not change saved volume or Settings previews.
