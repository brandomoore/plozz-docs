# Organize channels and fix listings

Find favorite [Live TV](/live-tv/) stations and repair missing listings without removing sources.

## Choose where Live TV opens

Under **Settings > Live TV > Live TV opens to**, choose **Categories** to pick a group first, or **Guide** for program listings.

**Channel order** chooses **Channel number** or **Name**. Under **Guide preferences**, **Recently watched** shows or hides that group. Choices stay with this profile on this device.

## Browse categories on iPhone and iPad

Choose a category or **All channels** for its guide. In narrow layouts, use **Category** to switch groups or **Categories** to return to the directory. Wider layouts keep a sidebar.

In the directory or sidebar, choose **Source**; **All** combines sources when offered. The directory's **More > Guide** opens all channels.

Open **More > Filters** to choose **Category**, **Language**, and **Country**, then optionally enable **Favorites only** or **Channels with a guide**. Under **Sort**, choose **Channel number** or **Name**. **Reset filters** clears filters and source selection, but keeps sorting and visibility.

## Wait for an IPTV guide

Keep Plozz open while your IPTV guide loads. Dismissing progress does not cancel it; percentages describe individual stages.

**Guide ready** means current listings were found, not that every channel has them. For **Guide update failed** or **Guide update paused**, open **TV Sources**, select the IPTV account's source, then **Refresh guide**. For **No guide configured**, add a guide address using **Edit connection** under **Settings > Sources**.

Use **Settings > Live TV > Guide preferences > Refresh while open** to update saved IPTV channel lists and guides regularly. It defaults to **Every 12 hours**; **Off** still updates when you use Live TV. The choice stays with this profile on this device. Updates need Plozz on screen and a previously used guide.

## Find hidden channels

Press and hold a channel for **Add to Favorites**, **Remove from Favorites**, or **Hide channel**.

On Apple TV, open **Manage channels** in Live TV's sidebar. On iPhone/iPad, open **More > Filters > Manage IPTV channels**, then:

- In **Categories**, **Show** overrides regional filtering; **Hide** hides that group; **Automatic** follows your regional choices.
- In **Countries and languages**, enable **Filter IPTV by country or language** and select the tags you want. A country OR language match is enough. Tags come from the playlist; Plozz does not guess from names.
- In **Hidden channels**, use **Show channel** to make an individual exception. **Reset category and region visibility** keeps individually hidden channels and scan exclusions.

Regional filtering can hide untagged channels. Check **Favorites only**, **Channels with a guide**, and your source too. Unavailable favorites stay saved.

## Tidy channel names

Under **Settings > Live TV > Channel names**, **Clean channel names** starts on and tidies IPTV symbols and spacing. Turn it off for original names. Check **Preview** (or **Example** without a sample). Edited names, guide matching, and favorites stay unchanged.

## Arrange and save a Multiview

Reveal [Multiview](/live-tv/#watch-several-channels) controls; on iPhone/iPad, choose **Edit layout** if needed.

**Layout** offers:

- **Side by side** shows two pictures; mobile portrait uses **Top and bottom**. More pictures use **Grid**.
- **Main picture** gives one picture more space.
- **Corner** overlays smaller pictures. Choose **Position** and **Size** to keep them away from something important.

Only one picture supplies sound: focus it on Apple TV or select it under **Audio** on mobile. To choose the main picture, hold it on Apple TV for **Make main video**; on mobile, select its Audio entry, then **More > Make main** while editing.

To change a channel, hold its picture on Apple TV for **Replace channel** or **Remove**. On mobile, select its **Audio** entry, then **More > Replace** or **More > Remove** while editing. Replacement opens the guide; removal keeps other pictures playing. One picture must remain.

Choose **More > Favorite** to save channels and layout for your profile. Reopen them from the guide's **Multiviews** (under **More** on narrow mobile layouts). Hold an entry for **Remove from Favorites**. Saving reserves no streams or tuners; missing channels may prevent reopening.

If favorites fail, choose **Multiviews > Retry**.

Multiview does not support AirPlay or Picture in Picture.

## Correct a channel's guide

These tools only support standalone playlists; IPTV accounts use **Refresh guide** and **Edit connection**.

1. Under a standalone playlist in **TV Sources**, open **Guide options > Correct guide mapping** when offered.
2. Select the channel, its guide source, and the correct station. Check the regional feed.

Corrections survive refreshes; **Use automatic matching** removes them.

Under **Guide options**, **Keep previous days** and **Look ahead** set how many days to keep. They cannot create missing listings; earlier listings are not recordings.

## Edit imported playlist details

In **TV Sources**, select the imported playlist, then **Playlist and guides**. Edit **Name (optional)** or guide addresses, then **Save source**. The file stays unchanged.

## Restore a saved channel identity

If **Review channel identities** appears in Guide options, inspect candidates before choosing **Restore saved identity**. Confirm only the same station and regional feed to carry over favorites, hiding, and guide corrections. Otherwise, leave it separate.

## Check unavailable links

In **TV Sources**, select a playlist, open **Check channel availability**, then **Scan channels**. This tests reachability, not playback-format support.

Open **Scan results**. Unavailable channels are hidden; uncertain results need another check. **Cancel scan** stops remaining checks.

Use **Restore channel** or **Restore hidden channels** to undo scan exclusions. Other hiding rules still apply. If saving fails, choose **Retry saving**; your choices are not yet saved.
