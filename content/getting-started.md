# Get started

Plozz plays media from your own sources on Apple TV, iPhone, and iPad.

## Set up Plozz

1. Join the [Plozz TestFlight beta](https://testflight.apple.com/join/EKfReNMu) and install it on a device running tvOS, iOS, or iPadOS 18 or later.
2. Open Plozz and choose your source: Jellyfin, Plex, Emby, Silo, IPTV, or **Media Share**.
3. [Connect your server](/servers/), [choose a network folder](/network-shares/), or [add your IPTV service](/live-tv/). Plozz supplies no movies, channels, or subscriptions.
4. Complete the profile, library, and appearance choices shown. A Plozz profile keeps your app preferences; it is separate from your server account.
5. Browse a library and choose a movie or episode to play; for channels, open **Live TV**.

Already use Plozz elsewhere? Choose **Set up from another device** during setup, then follow [Profiles and sync](/profiles-and-sync/). Silo still requires approval for each device.

## While watching

Use the player controls for [audio and versions](/playback/) or [subtitles](/subtitles/). On iPhone and iPad, you can also [download videos for offline viewing](/downloads/).

Need help? Start with [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/) or the [Plozz community](https://plozz.app/discord).
