# Get started

Plozz plays media from your own sources on Apple TV, iPhone, and iPad.

## Set up Plozz

1. **Install Plozz.** Download [Plozz Player from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/plozz-player/id6782668147) for tvOS, iOS or iPadOS 18 or later.
2. **Choose whether to sync.** If prompted, choose **Enable iCloud Sync** to restore an existing setup, or **Not now** to start locally. You can change this in Settings.
3. **Choose a source.** Choose Jellyfin, Plex, Emby, Silo, IPTV, or **Media Share**. Beta builds also offer [Navidrome music](/servers/#connect-navidrome-or-subsonic).
4. **Connect your media.** [Connect a server](/servers/), [choose a network folder](/network-shares/), or [add IPTV](/live-tv/). Plozz supplies no media or subscriptions.
5. **Make it yours.** Complete the profile, library, and appearance choices. [Plozz profiles](/profiles-and-locks/) keep app preferences separately from server accounts.
6. **Start watching.** [Browse or search](/library/), or open **Live TV** for channels.

If **Help improve Plozz?** appears, choose **Share Diagnostics** or **Don't Share**. Both continue setup; you can change [diagnostics sharing](/feedback-and-bugs/#choose-diagnostics-sharing) later.

While libraries load, **Choose later** continues setup; return to **Settings > Libraries** afterward. For failed loads, use **Try Again** and check the server user.

> [!TIP]
> Already use Plozz? [Restore through iCloud](/profiles-and-sync/#restore-an-existing-setup), or choose **Set up from another device** on Apple TV or **Set Up from Another Device** on iPhone/iPad.

## Find a setting

Select your profile before changing **Settings**. On Apple TV, use the **Search settings** field. On iPhone/iPad, use the settings search field.

Enter a setting or task, then select a result. Plozz opens its page and focuses the control on Apple TV, or briefly highlights it on mobile.

Some controls need a feature enabled first; search can take you to that prerequisite instead. Search does not turn it on or bypass a Parental PIN.

## Make the setup yours

Choose [Home rows and shortcuts](/home-and-navigation/) or [cards, artwork, and language](/appearance/). Apple TV can [remember your profile](/apple-tv-profiles/).

For children, configure [parental controls](/parental-controls/); hiding a row is not access control.

## While watching

Use [Player controls](/playback/) to choose [audio](/audio/) or [subtitle tracks](/subtitles/). Before travelling with iPhone/iPad, [download and test an offline copy](/downloads/).

For help, see [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/) or [Feedback and bug reports](/feedback-and-bugs/).
