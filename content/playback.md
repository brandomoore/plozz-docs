# Player controls

Reveal the controls to change playback without leaving the video. Options depend on the active session.

## Reach the controls

On Apple TV, press **Up** from the scrub surface for Playback, Audio, and Subtitles. Press **Down** for the information area and available episode or playlist navigation. On iPhone/iPad, tap the video and open its options.

Use the timeline to seek. Apple TV's **Seek without pausing** controls whether you must pause first; [device controls](/platform-controls/) explains remote, touch, and keyboard differences.

## Choose audio or a version

Open **Audio** for tracks or **Version** for files and editions. On iPhone/iPad, both are in playback options.

Version switching stays on the active server/account and resumes at your current position. Different cuts can have different timelines.

The first multi-version video asks how to choose. Change this later in **Settings > Playback > Version selection**:

- **Ask every time:** choose before each multi-version video, including autoplay.
- **Choose automatically:** use a remembered choice or a device-recommended version without opening the picker.

**Remember series version choices** reuses the kind of version you selected for later episodes. On Apple TV, its switch is **Remember per series**. Turn it off to ignore series choices. In automatic mode, a missing match uses the best available version rather than asking again.

## Autoplay and skipping

Under **Settings > Playback**, set autoplay separately for the next episode and next playlist item. Both default to on. **Show Up Next card** controls the shortcut card separately: leaving the card on does not require automatic playback.

Its lead time controls when it appears without a credits marker.

**Skip intros, credits & more** can show a button or skip automatically when markers exist. You can set different behavior for intros, credits, recaps, previews, and commercials.

**Use community markers** is enabled by default. It looks up missing intro/credits markers using the title's IMDb or TMDB identifier, sending that identifier to IntroDB/TheIntroDB. Disable it to stop these lookups. Marker availability is not guaranteed.

**Rewind on resume** (**Resume rewind** on iPhone/iPad) starts before the saved position: five seconds by default, adjustable from zero to 60 seconds. This is not necessarily lost progress.

## Speed, picture, and other navigation

Use playback speed and zoom where offered. Fit keeps the full picture; Fill can crop it.

Episode/playlist navigation is available, but there is no chapter picker. Intro markers are not chapter browsing.

See [Streaming quality](/streaming-quality/), [Audio](/audio/), or [Subtitles](/subtitles/) for their settings. [AirPlay and Picture in Picture](/platform-controls/#airplay-and-picture-in-picture) have additional limits.
