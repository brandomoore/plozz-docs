# Player controls

Resume a video, choose an edition, or skip intros.

## Reach the controls

On Apple TV, press up from the timeline for available **Audio**, **Subtitles**, speed, and **More** controls; press down for information and navigation.

On iPhone/iPad, tap the video. Portrait uses the ellipsis **Playback options** menu. Landscape separates Audio, Subtitles, and speed, with other options in the ellipsis **Playback settings** menu.

See [device controls](/platform-controls/) or [disc menus](/disc-playback/).

## Keep controls visible

Open **Settings > Playback > Auto-hide controls**. **While playing**, the default, keeps controls visible when paused. Choose **While playing or paused** for an unobstructed paused picture, or **Never** to keep controls visible until you hide them yourself.

VoiceOver and Switch Control prevent automatic hiding in the movie/episode player.

## Seek on Apple TV

Close playback options, then click the remote's left/right edge to jump by your skip interval. Swipe to scrub; **Seek without pausing** determines whether to pause first.

Hold a left/right click to start scanning, which continues after release. Click the same direction to speed up, or the opposite to slow down and eventually reverse.

Press the center button to seek, or play/pause to seek and resume. Back cancels. Wait for loading before seeking.

## Check when a video will finish

- On Apple TV, close menus. With the timeline visible, lightly tap the remote without clicking for clock times. Another tap hides controls. If hidden, a light tap reveals them first.
- On iPhone/iPad, tap either time beside the timeline. VoiceOver calls these **Elapsed time** and **Remaining time**. Tap again to switch back.

The estimate needs a known duration and accounts for playback speed. Revealing hidden controls returns to elapsed and remaining times.

## Choose audio or a version

Open **Audio** for tracks. For files/editions, choose **More > Version** on Apple TV, or **Version** in **Playback options**/**Playback settings** on iPhone/iPad.

Changing versions keeps your server account and position; different cuts may not line up.

The first multi-version video asks your preference. Change **Settings > Playback > Version selection**:

- **Ask every time**: choose before multi-version videos, including autoplay.
- **Choose automatically**: use a remembered or device-recommended version without the picker.
- **Use first from server**: follow the selected server's version order, ignoring remembered preferences unless you explicitly choose a version.

**How automatic selection works** explains how files suit your device, not current connection speed.

**Remember series version choices** reuses your version type for later episodes in automatic mode, or preselects it when asked. Turn it off to stop reusing it. Automatic mode chooses an alternative if that type is missing.

## Resume or start over

Enable **Settings > Playback > Ask to resume or start over** to choose before resuming. **Resume** shows time remaining; **Start Over** shows full runtime, when known.

**Rewind on resume** starts five seconds before your saved position by default. Choose zero to resume exactly, or up to 60 seconds for more context.

## Autoplay and skipping

Under **Settings > Playback**, next-episode and next-playlist-item autoplay default on. **Show Up Next card** controls the preview separately; its lead time applies without credits markers.

Enable **Skip intros, credits & more**, then open **Customize**. Choose **Default behavior**: **Show skip button**, **Skip instantly**, or **Skip after** (1-30 seconds). Select movies, TV shows, or anime under **Enable for**.

Open an intro, credits, recap, preview, or commercial marker's settings to change its behavior by content category, including **Off**. **Use default** restores the shared behavior. Enable disabled categories on the previous page first. Skip actions need timings.

With skipping enabled, **Use community markers** (default on) checks IntroDB/TheIntroDB if intros or credits are missing. Lookups send IMDb/TMDB/TVDB identifiers and applicable episode numbers, ordering, name, and runtime, not video files. Disable it to stop community lookups, not server markers. Coverage and matching vary.

## Jump to a chapter

Open **Chapters** below the timeline and choose a chapter; paused playback stays paused. Mobile tabs scroll horizontally. Chapters need server or file support; some videos lack them or their images. Intro and credits markers are separate.

## Switch to another Continue Watching title

Open **Continue Watching** below the timeline to resume another title. On Apple TV, press down; on iPhone/iPad, scroll the tabs if needed.

Choose a card to save your position and resume that title after access or version prompts.

It uses Home's loaded titles, excluding this video and its show's episodes from that source. It hides when none remain.

Under **Settings > Playback > Player tabs**, choose which of **Chapters**, **Cast**, **Episodes**, **Playlist**, and **Continue Watching** to show. A tab appears only when it has content; **Info** remains available.

## Identify a song in a movie or show

In **Info**, select a recognized song card for **Open song**. [Song identification](/identify-songs/) explains setup, services, and privacy.

## Speed, picture, and other navigation

Select the current-rate **Playback Speed** button; mobile portrait places it under **Playback options**. On Apple TV, adjust **Speed**. Mobile offers presets and **Custom…** for finer control. Choose 1x for normal speed.

For framing, open **More > Zoom Mode** on Apple TV, or **Zoom** in mobile **Playback options**/**Playback settings**. See [Video framing and upscaling](/video-picture/) for cropping and enhancement.

## Explore the cast

In **Cast**, select a person for their biography and credits without leaving the video. **See more** opens their full page; a credit opens that title's details. Either ends playback. Listed credits may be outside your library.
