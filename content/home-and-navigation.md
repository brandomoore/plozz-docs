# Home and navigation

Keep favorite rows and shortcuts within reach.

## Choose the hero

Use **Settings > Screens & Navigation > Home > Hero** to change the featured carousel.

On Apple TV, choose **On** or **Off**; on mobile, use **Show hero**. Hiding it preserves rows and row style.

With the hero on, open **Content sources** on Apple TV or **Hero content sources** on mobile. Choose **Continue Watching**, **Recently Added**, **Watchlist**, **Random from Library**, **Collections**, or **Discovery**. Set **Items to show**, **Advance automatically**, and **Ratings**. **Trailers & music** opens [preview choices](/trailers-and-previews/#choose-trailers-and-previews).

Enable Discovery to choose **Discovery providers**. Suggestions can be outside your library; requests need Seerr. Kids profiles exclude Discovery; [Discover filters](/discover/) are separate.

## Choose row style on Apple TV

In **Home**, open **Row style**. **Showcase** fills the background with the focused title; **Classic** keeps separate rows. Either works without the hero.

**Showcase** shares the hero's rating choices. With the hero off, its **Discovery row** switch reveals **Discovery providers**.

## Choose Home rows

Open **Settings > Screens & Navigation > Home > Rows**.

Under **Preset**, choose **Match server** to follow available server rows, adapting as sources change, or **Combined rows** to group libraries. Some rows stay separate. Confirming replaces your Home arrangement, not server settings. Plex follows promoted rows, not another client's pins.

Choose **Add row**, or open a row for **Remove from Home**. Choose **Edit**, move rows, then **Done**; holding a row also offers **Reorder**. Manual arrangement changes select **Custom**, stopping automatic adaptation. Reselect Match server to resume it.

Removing rows does not restrict access. Unavailable rows stay saved: open **Details**, check the source and server user, then **Retry**. **Section unavailable in the latest refresh** can mean an empty or changed server row.

## Clear an empty-row message

In **Classic** or mobile Home, **No titles to show** means nothing matched your settings.

**Dismiss** hides the message, not future titles; **Customize Home** opens settings. To restore it, select the row under **Home > Rows**, then **Show empty message**. Showcase omits these messages.

## Add a collection section

Choose **Add row > Custom row**, then [select collections and a layout](/custom-home-rows/).

## Rename rows and shortcuts

Under **Home > Rows**, open a row and edit **Row name** (**Name** for custom rows). For shortcuts, open **App navigation > Hide or Reorder Navigation**, hold one, choose **Rename**, then edit Name.

On Apple TV, closing the name keyboard saves, even with Back; for **Couldn't save**, choose **Retry**. On mobile, use the **Save changes** checkmark. Leave the name blank to restore its default.

Home-row names can [sync](/profiles-and-sync/). Shortcut names stay local, except Watchlist uses its Home-row name.

## Keep older Continue Watching titles

Continue Watching, including merged Next Up, does not remove titles because of their age or the row's size. Your sources' history, watched status, dismissals, and server access still matter; [shares have a storage limit](/network-shares/#keep-your-place). Disabling library browsing does not hide account-wide history.

Separate Next Up rows preview up to 20 titles; [open the larger grid](/browse-home-rows/) if offered.

Brief plays can save progress without adding a card. Watch one minute, or 5% of videos under 20 minutes with a five-second minimum. Trailers stay out.

Under **Rows > Continue Watching**, turn off **Include Next Up** for separate rows; it defaults on. Removing Continue Watching also separates Next Up; **Continue Watching settings** remains available.

If refreshing fails, older cards can remain. Select the exclamation-mark **Refresh details** button beside Continue Watching's heading, then **Try Again**. This reloads the row without resetting your history; see [when Home refreshes](/library/#catch-up-after-watching-elsewhere).

## Hide watched Home suggestions

Under **Home > Filtering > Hide watched content**, choose independently:

- **Recently Added**: hides completed titles. It defaults off; in-progress videos and shows with unwatched episodes remain.
- **Hero & discovery**: hides watched hero and Discovery suggestions. It defaults on.

These affect suggestions, not history, access, Browse, or Continue Watching. Recently Added checks a limited selection; turn its filter off if too few titles appear.

## Arrange shortcuts and details

Open **Settings > Screens & Navigation > App navigation**. On Apple TV, choose a layout:

| Layout | How to reach destinations |
| --- | --- |
| **Pinned Sidebar** | The default left-hand icon rail expands when focused. |
| **Sidebar** | The native sidebar opens from the top left. |
| **Top Bar** | Destinations scroll across the top. |

Leave Settings to apply. All support libraries and [All Libraries](/combined-libraries/).

Use **Hide or Reorder Navigation** to arrange shortcuts; drag on mobile. **Reset Navigation** restores order/visibility, not names, library tabs, or access.

Navigation stays local to this profile and device.

Apple TV sidebars always retain **Settings**; if hidden in Top Bar, use the profile picker.

On mobile, Home, Upcoming, Downloads, and Search start shown when relevant content is available. Libraries and **All Libraries** start **Hidden**: hold one and choose **Show**. Seerr can reveal Discover.

If mobile **Settings** is missing, use your profile avatar on **Home** or a library.

Compact native navigation puts extra visible shortcuts under **More**, not hidden ones. On iOS/iPadOS 26 or later, setting **Appearance > Liquid Glass** to **Off** can instead show an opaque bottom strip: scroll it sideways for extra shortcuts. The system's Reduce Transparency setting keeps native navigation.

Under **Screens & Navigation > Movie & show details**, choose [Trailers & music](/trailers-and-previews/#choose-trailers-and-previews), scores, and age recommendations.

See [library customization](/customize-libraries/), [appearance](/appearance/), and [Continue Watching artwork](/spoiler-protection/#change-continue-watching-cards).
