# Browse more from Home

Found a useful recommendation row? Open its larger grid to look beyond the Home preview, compare titles, or find something unwatched without searching the whole library.

## Open a larger grid

1. Open **Home** in the Plozz profile you want to use.
2. On Apple TV, move to the end of an eligible row and select **See all**. On iPhone/iPad, tap the row heading with a chevron.
3. Scroll through the grid, then select a title to open its details.

Loaded rows with more titles can offer a larger grid: **Recently Added**, supported server recommendations, and [custom rows](/custom-home-rows/). Loading, empty, or failed rows do not offer that grid. A collection heading's separate **See all** link can still open the collection when its preview is empty or unavailable.

The Home **Watchlist** shortcut opens the regular [Watchlist page](/watchlist/#filter-and-sort-the-page), with its saved browse preferences.

## Edit a Home row on iPhone/iPad

Hold a row heading on Home and choose **Edit** to open that row's settings. This is a shortcut to [renaming or changing the row](/home-and-navigation/#rename-rows-and-shortcuts), not editing your server's library. Tapping the chevron still opens the larger grid.

If **Setting unavailable** appears after your profile or available rows change, return to Home and try again.

## Narrow or rearrange the results

Apple TV places filtering before sorting above the grid. On iPhone/iPad, use the sort and filter icons at the top right.

- Open the filter menu. Under **Media**, choose **All**, **Movies**, or **TV Shows**. Under **Watch status**, choose **All**, **Unwatched**, or **In Progress**. The two choices work together; **Clear Filters** resets both.
- Open the sort menu. Under **Sort By**, choose **Name**, **Date Added**, **Release Date**, or **Runtime**; change **Order** if needed. **Source order** restores the source's ordering and hides Order.

Changing filters or sorting may need to load the rest of the row before showing results. Wait for loading to finish. Custom rows start with their saved choices, but changes here do not rearrange Home or change your library's saved browse settings.

## When the grid is empty or fails

For **No titles to show**, use **Clear Filters** if offered, or choose **All** in both pickers. For a loading error, choose **Retry**.

The grid follows that row's list and your server access, not the entire library catalogue. Server-supplied lists can still be limited. To look elsewhere, open a library's [Browse view](/library/#browse-a-library). To change which rows appear, use [Home settings](/home-and-navigation/#choose-home-rows).
