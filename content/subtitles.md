# Subtitles

Choose subtitles in the player, or save appearance and automatic-selection preferences in Settings.

## Choose a track

1. Reveal the playback controls and open **Subtitles**.
2. Select a listed track, or **Off**.
3. For two tracks with a movie or episode, use **Style > Dual Subtitles** on Apple TV, or **Dual Subtitles** if shown directly. On iPhone/iPad, use **Second Track**.
4. If supported by the connected server, choose **Download subtitles…** on Apple TV or **Search for Subtitles** on iPhone/iPad, then select a result.

Search depends on the server’s configured subtitle services. Network shares use subtitles already present with your media, without server-based search.

Second tracks require eligible text subtitles. An image-based primary disables the second track, and image subtitles cannot be restyled.

## Change appearance

Open **Settings > Subtitles** and choose **Customize subtitle style**—under **Subtitle style** on Apple TV.

Preview changes to text size, font, color, background, and position. In the player, choose **Style** on Apple TV, or **Appearance > Style** on iPhone/iPad.

For a different Live TV appearance, enable **Use a separate style for Live TV** in Settings > Subtitles.

## Set automatic choices

Settings > Subtitles also controls when subtitles appear, preferred language, automatic downloads, and SDH preferences for captions with sound descriptions. Remembered per-series choices, including Off, can override automatic rules.

Where offered, set subtitle behavior separately for movies, TV shows, and anime. Forced-subtitle preferences are separate from SDH: forced tracks cover selected dialogue or signs, while SDH includes sound descriptions. Neither choice creates a track the file or subtitle service does not have.

To stop reusing a series' choice, turn off **Remember subtitles per series** in **Settings > Subtitles** on Apple TV, or under **Tracks** in **Settings > Playback** on iPhone/iPad.

For mistimed text, use the **Subtitle Sync** clock button on Apple TV or **Playback Sync > Subtitle Delay** in iPhone/iPad playback options, when available.

## Missing, wrong, or drifting subtitles

Check the selected video version, subtitle language, and remembered series choice first. For an online search failure, check the server's subtitle-service configuration rather than changing Plozz's appearance.

A steady timing offset may be correctable with delay. Increasing drift can mean the subtitle belongs to another release or cut; try another matching track instead of repeatedly increasing the offset.

If a style control or second track is unavailable, check whether the primary is image-based. If captions disappear on an external screen, check the current [AirPlay limitation](/platform-controls/).
