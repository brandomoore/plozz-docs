# Discover movies and shows

Find popular and upcoming movies and shows, including titles outside your libraries.

## Open Discover

Choose **Discover** in the main navigation. If it is missing:

1. Open **Settings > Screens & Navigation > App navigation > Hide or Reorder Navigation**.
2. Press and hold **Discover**, then choose **Show**.
3. Return to browsing and open Discover. On iPhone/iPad, check **More** in compact native navigation, or scroll the opaque bottom strip sideways.

A Seerr connection can show Discover automatically; without one, it normally starts hidden. Kids profiles do not offer Discover.

## Find something to watch

Browse **Trending**, **Popular Movies**, **Upcoming Movies**, **Popular Series**, and **Upcoming Series**, in that order. Move along a row to load more titles.

Open a title to see its details. An accessible library copy can play normally. You can save missing titles to your [Watchlist](/watchlist/) or [request them through Seerr](/media-requests/) when those actions are offered. Upcoming release information is not a playable file or a promised addition.

## Narrow the recommendations

Open the funnel menu at the top, labeled **All** on Apple TV:

- **Available now** keeps titles Plozz can match to a playable copy in your enabled libraries. It also hides both Upcoming rows. A title marked available in Seerr may still be inaccessible to your Plozz profile; check the source connection if an expected title is missing.
- **Hide watched** removes titles Plozz knows you have watched.

Both start off and are saved for your profile. **Clear Filters** restores the full selection; these filters are separate from Home's hero filters.

## If a row will not load

Choose **Try Again** beside the failed row. For an empty filtered row, use **Clear Filters**.

Seerr is not required just to browse. If you have connected it, check that connection when recommendations fail. Otherwise, check your [metadata settings](/metadata-and-ratings/).
