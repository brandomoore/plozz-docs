# Identify a song while watching

Find a song you hear in a movie or show, then look it up in a music service without typing its name.

## Find and open a song

Song identification starts on for your Plozz profile. Check **Settings > Playback > Audio > Identify songs** if you have turned it off. It needs internet and supported in-app playback; on iPhone/iPad, turn **Offline Mode** off.

1. Reveal the player controls and open **Info** while the song plays.
2. When a song card appears, select it. **Open song** shows the song and artist with service choices.
3. Choose **Search Spotify**, **Apple Music** when available, **Search YouTube Music**, **Search Amazon Music**, **Search Deezer**, or **Search TIDAL**.

Apple Music opens the matched song when a link is available. The other choices search for its title and artist; check the results for the right recording.

On Apple TV, Plozz tries a compatible installed app. If it cannot open the link, scan the displayed QR code with your phone. On iPhone/iPad, the link opens an app or browser. Each service's sign-in, subscription, and regional availability still apply. **Done** closes the chooser.

## If no song appears

Identification uses Shazam and the audio Plozz is playing, not your microphone. Apple's native player and some server-converted streams cannot supply that audio. A missing match means no song card, not a playback error.

Turn off **Identify songs** to stop recognition. Opening a service search sends the song's title and artist to that service; it does not add music to your Plozz library.
