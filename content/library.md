# Find and organize media

Start in a library to browse what your connected account can access. **Search** finds movies, shows, and episodes across your available sources.

With [Seerr](/media-requests/), **Not in Your Library** search results are requestable titles, not playable copies. Request permissions still apply.

## Browse a library

1. Open the library from navigation, or open **All Libraries**.
2. Use **Browse** for the catalogue rather than recommendations.
3. Open the filter and sort controls. Available fields depend on the source; clear filters if a title disappears.
4. Open a show, choose its season, and select an episode. Collections and playlists appear when the source supplies them.

On Apple TV, name-sorted libraries can show an A-Z letter rail; other sorts and collection-member lists do not.

While **Checking collections...** is shown, visible collections are only the checked results, not necessarily the complete list. If loading is interrupted, choose **Try Again**. For failures with **Details**, include that report when [asking for help](/troubleshooting/#report-a-problem).

Press and hold a card on Apple TV, or touch and hold on iPhone/iPad, for actions. **Go to Show**, **Go to Movie**, and **Go to Episode** open details. A share's **Browse Files** shows original folders.

Scroll down details for cast, related titles, and **Extras** exposed by the source. Where offered, use the server/version selector to choose a playable copy; on Apple TV, open the header's **More actions** button.

## Save titles and manage history

Use **Add to Watchlist** on a movie or show, then open **Watchlist** for this profile. **Remove from Watchlist** undoes it.

Watchlist combines Plozz's saved titles with supported connected-server and [tracker](/trackers/) lists, including WeTrakr's planning list. Adds/removals can propagate to those services: check the server user and tracker account first. An entry does not make an unavailable title playable.

**Mark as Watched**, **Mark as Unwatched**, and **Mark Watched Up to Here** change history where supported. The last action includes earlier episodes: use it carefully.

**Remove from Continue Watching**, when offered, clears the resume point without claiming you finished the title. It is not the same as removing a Watchlist entry.

## A title or library is missing

Check the active [profile and server user](/profiles-and-locks/), then **Settings > Libraries**. A disabled library is different from a hidden navigation shortcut or [Home row](/home-and-navigation/). Clear browse filters before changing accounts. For network folders, check the [scan and content type](/network-shares/); for incorrect artwork or descriptions, see [Metadata and ratings](/metadata-and-ratings/).
