# Find and organize media

Browse the libraries your connected account can access. **Search** finds movies, shows, and episodes across your available sources.

With [Seerr](/media-requests/), **Not in Your Library** results are requestable, not playable; permissions apply. Use [Discover](/discover/) for popular and upcoming titles.

## Browse a library

1. Open a library, or [All Libraries](/combined-libraries/).
2. Use **Browse** for the catalogue. [Customize library pages](/customize-libraries/) to arrange tabs and Recommended rows.
3. Filter or sort where supported. **Genre** and **Year** narrow results together with the selected quick filter; **Clear Filters** resets all three.
4. Open a show, choose its season, then select an episode. Collections and playlists appear when supplied by the source.

Apple TV's name-sorted Browse can show an [A-Z rail](/combined-libraries/#jump-through-a-large-library); other sorts and collection-member lists do not.

Some choices show **Preparing library filter…** before the results are ready. Wait, or choose **Cancel**; neither changes your library. If filter options fail, choose **Couldn't load filter options. Try Again**.

**Checking collections...** means results are incomplete. Choose **Try Again** after interruption; include **Details** when [reporting failures](/troubleshooting/#report-a-problem).

Press and hold a card on Apple TV, or touch and hold on iPhone/iPad, for actions. **Go to Show**, **Go to Movie**, and **Go to Episode** open details; a share's **Browse Files** opens original folders.

Choose a copy with the detail page's server/version selector, when offered; Apple TV uses the header's **More actions**.

Upcoming dates and unaired placeholders are schedule information, not playable files or promised additions.

Network shares can hold [streaming-service shortcuts](/network-shares/#open-links-to-streaming-services).

## Read more about a title

Below the videos and cast, read **About**, **Ratings**, [Release & Availability](/media-requests/#read-release-and-availability-information), **Details**, **Crew**, and **Playback**, when available. Playback describes the selected file, not measured connection speed. **Total Seasons** and **Total Episodes** count the whole show, not your available or unwatched episodes.

Select a shortened description or a field marked **MORE** to read its full information. On iPhone/iPad, **Done** closes an expanded group; the full-description reader has a close button. On Apple TV, press Back.

Cast members and related titles open their own pages. A listed credit is not necessarily in your library.

## Watch a trailer

On details, **Trailer** plays a server file in Plozz; **Watch on YouTube** opens an external link when allowed. [Trailers and previews](/trailers-and-previews/) explains source choices, autoplay, sound, and Apple TV's full-screen preview controls.

## Watch extras

For behind-the-scenes clips or other bonus videos, scroll to **Extras** on the title's details and select a video. If that row fails to load, use its **Try Again** button.

Extras belong to the selected source: another copy may offer different videos. The row disappears when the source returns no extras. Reopen an extra to resume when its source supports it. Plex can keep recent extra progress for this profile and server user on this device; older positions can be dropped as that local history fills.

Watching an extra or trailer does not mark the main movie or episode watched, copy progress to matching titles on other servers, or send a watch to connected trackers. Trailers never appear in Continue Watching.

## Save titles and manage history

Use **Add to Watchlist** on a movie/show. [Watchlist](/watchlist/) explains connected-list effects, filtering, sorting, and missing entries; saving grants no playback access.

**Mark as Watched**, **Mark as Unwatched**, and **Mark Watched Up to Here** change supported history. The last includes earlier episodes and, on Apple TV, earlier seasons; use carefully.

**Remove from Continue Watching** preserves position where dismissal is supported; otherwise it clears resume. It neither marks watched nor removes Watchlist entries. [Cross-server history](/profiles-and-sync/#keep-watch-history-separate) governs matching copies.

## A title or library is missing

Check the [profile and server user](/profiles-and-locks/), **Settings > Libraries**, and filters. Disabled libraries differ from hidden shortcuts or [Home rows](/home-and-navigation/). For shares, check [scanning and content type](/network-shares/); for wrong artwork or descriptions, see [Metadata and ratings](/metadata-and-ratings/).
