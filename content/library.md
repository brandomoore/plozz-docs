# Find and organize media

Start in a library to browse what your connected account can access. **Search** finds movies, shows, and episodes across your available sources.

With [Seerr](/media-requests/), **Not in Your Library** results are requestable, not playable; request permissions apply.

## Browse a library

1. Open a library, or [All Libraries](/combined-libraries/).
2. Use **Browse** for the catalogue. [Customize library pages](/customize-libraries/) to arrange tabs and Recommended rows.
3. Filter or sort where supported. **Genre** and **Year** narrow results together with the selected quick filter; **Clear Filters** resets all three.
4. Open a show, choose its season, then select an episode. Collections and playlists appear when supplied by the source.

Apple TV's name-sorted Browse can show an [A-Z rail](/combined-libraries/#jump-through-a-large-library); other sorts and collection-member lists do not.

Some choices need **Preparing library filter…** before showing results. Wait, or choose **Cancel**; this does not change your library. If filter options fail, choose **Try Again**.

**Checking collections...** means results are incomplete. Choose **Try Again** after interruption; include **Details** when [reporting failures](/troubleshooting/#report-a-problem).

Press and hold a card on Apple TV, or touch and hold on iPhone/iPad, for actions. **Go to Show**, **Go to Movie**, and **Go to Episode** open details; a share's **Browse Files** opens original folders.

Detail pages include cast, related titles, and source-supplied **Extras**. Choose a copy with the server/version selector, when offered; Apple TV uses the header's **More actions**.

Upcoming dates and unaired placeholders are schedule information, not playable files or promised additions.

## Watch a trailer

On details, **Trailer** plays an accessible server-owned file in Plozz. These files also supply automatic Home/detail previews. **Watch on YouTube** opens an online link outside Plozz, independently of your external-player preference.

Apple TV needs the YouTube app; iPhone/iPad can fall back to its website. For **Couldn't open YouTube**, check that app. YouTube controls video availability and restrictions. Missing trailer metadata can leave no button; a visible link does not guarantee playback.

## Save titles and manage history

Use **Add to Watchlist** on a movie/show. [Watchlist](/watchlist/) explains connected-list effects, filtering, sorting, and missing entries; saving grants no playback access.

**Mark as Watched**, **Mark as Unwatched**, and **Mark Watched Up to Here** change supported history. The last includes earlier episodes and, on Apple TV, earlier seasons; use carefully.

**Remove from Continue Watching** preserves position where dismissal is supported; otherwise it clears resume. It neither marks watched nor removes Watchlist entries. [Cross-server history](/profiles-and-sync/#keep-watch-history-separate) governs matching copies.

## A title or library is missing

Check the [profile and server user](/profiles-and-locks/), **Settings > Libraries**, and filters. Disabled libraries differ from hidden shortcuts or [Home rows](/home-and-navigation/). For shares, check [scanning and content type](/network-shares/); for wrong artwork or descriptions, see [Metadata and ratings](/metadata-and-ratings/).
