# Appearance and language

Choose readable cards and artwork in **Settings > Appearance**.

## Make cards easier to read

In **Theme**, choose colors and gradients. **Liquid Glass > Off** uses solid backgrounds; you can also follow system Reduce Transparency.

Open **Cards > Labels** and choose **App default**, **Show labels everywhere**, or **Hide labels everywhere**. A preset replaces your individual choices.

Open **Customize labels > Page** for finer control. Home and Recommended have **Default** and row **Overrides**. **Mixed** hides labels on series artwork and in Apple TV's Showcase.

Continue Watching's **Use default** follows Home or Recommended; **On** or **Off** overrides both. Home's **Libraries** override controls library-card labels.

Titles inside collections/playlists use **Browse**, not [cover labels](/customize-libraries/#choose-collection-cover-shapes). Under **Screens & Navigation**, **Home > Card labels** and **Customize labels** in **Libraries** or **Movie & show details** edit these same preferences.

On Apple TV, choose **Page > Movie & show details > Only show descriptions on focus** to show only the focused episode's synopsis. Episode labels must be on; spoiler protection still applies.

## Read watch indicators

Under **Cards > Watch indicators**, **Unwatched** marks unstarted titles with a corner flag; **Watched** marks completed titles with a check. **Hidden** removes those marks, not episode counts or progress.

With **Show unwatched episode count** on, choose **Episode count style**. **Show progress bars** is separate; hiding bars preserves progress and history. These choices [sync with your profile](/profiles-and-sync/).

**Display Size** changes card size, columns, and spacing, not video resolution.

## Choose artwork

In **Artwork**, presets replace your custom choices:

- **Recommended**: Plozz chooses artwork for each area.
- **Prefer my library's artwork**: use library images first, with online fallback when missing.
- **Prefer artwork from metadata providers**: online images first, with library fallback. Extra lookups can slow browsing.

Choose **Customize by view**, then **Library** or **Metadata providers** for each area. **Title detail pages** also offers **Mixed**: online backgrounds and logos with library posters for related titles. Home's Watchlist row and page are separate. Existing downloads keep their images.

[Metadata Providers](/metadata-and-ratings/) settings are shared; artwork choices are per-profile.

## Resize title logos

Under **Settings > Screens & Navigation**, adjust **Logo size** and **Text size** in **Home > Hero** (with the hero enabled) and **Movie & show details**. Apple TV's **Home > Row style > Showcase** has separate sizes; [library Recommended headers](/customize-libraries/#adjust-the-apple-tv-library-header) are independent too.

Text size changes titles, descriptions, metadata, and Apple TV episode text, not cards/menus. The center is default; tight spaces may use smaller sizes.

## Change language or nighttime appearance

**Settings > Language** changes interface language, separately from [audio](/audio/) and [subtitle language](/subtitles/).

**System** follows device languages without translating server information. Dates and numbers follow device region; system prompts retain device language.

See [Spoiler protection](/spoiler-protection/), [Circadian Mode](/circadian-mode/), and [Home/navigation](/home-and-navigation/).
