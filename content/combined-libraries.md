# Browse libraries together

**All Libraries** brings enabled video libraries into one grid on Apple TV, iPhone, and iPad, including libraries from different connected servers. It does not move media or combine server accounts.

## Open All Libraries on Apple TV

1. Enable the libraries under **Settings > Libraries** for the active Plozz profile.
2. Open **All Libraries** from navigation. It is offered when more than one browsable library is available.
3. If the shortcut is hidden, open **Settings > Screens & Navigation > App navigation > Hide or Reorder Navigation** and show it. All three Apple TV navigation layouts support library shortcuts.
4. Browse, sort, or filter the grid.

## Open All Libraries on iPhone or iPad

1. Enable at least two browsable libraries under **Settings > Libraries**.
2. Open **Settings > Screens & Navigation > App navigation > Hide or Reorder Navigation**. Find **All Libraries** under **Hidden**, then hold it and choose **Show**. Individual library shortcuts can be shown here too.
3. Open **All Libraries** from navigation; check **More** on compact layouts.

Mobile library shortcuts start hidden until you show them. Hiding a shortcut again does not disable its libraries.

## Choose among copies

Movies and shows can appear together; music libraries are excluded. Matching titles from several sources can share one card. Matching depends on the available identity information, so not every duplicate is guaranteed to merge.

Open the title and use its server/version selector where offered. On Apple TV, this is in the header's **More actions** menu. Each copy still belongs to its original server and must be accessible to your profile.

**All Libraries** is separate from [combined Home rows](/home-and-navigation/). Changing Home's arrangement does not enable or disable this grid.

## Jump through a large library

On Apple TV, sort a library's **Browse** view by name, then scroll a few rows down to reveal the right-hand letter rail when an index is available. Move focus onto a letter to jump to its titles. This also works in All Libraries. Other sorts and collection-member lists do not expose the index.

A deep jump can need more loading, especially with Silo or combined libraries. Wait for the destination to load. If a jump fails, select the letter again. If the index fails to load, change to another sort and back to name to rebuild it; an error does not mean that letter has no titles.

> [!NOTE]
> iPhone and iPad have All Libraries, but not Apple TV's letter rail. Use filters, scrolling, or **Search** instead.

## Results are missing

Check the active profile, enabled libraries, filters, and each server's connection. A hidden shortcut is not a disabled library. See [Find and organize media](/library/#a-title-or-library-is-missing).
