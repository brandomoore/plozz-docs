# Play Blu-ray folders

Watch the main video from a Blu-ray folder on a [network share](/network-shares/), or use its supported menu to explore other videos. Keep the complete, decrypted folder together; Plozz does not decrypt protected discs.

## Choose how a disc opens

1. Add the folder's parent as a **Media Share** library and [scan it](/network-shares/#find-files-and-refresh). Plozz recognizes a BDMV folder with `index.bdmv`, `PLAYLIST`, and `STREAM`, rather than listing its clips as separate movies.
2. Open the title's details and select the disc version if other copies are offered.
3. Start playback. **Play video** is the default and uses normal saved progress.

To prefer menus for this profile, open **Settings > Playback > Discs > Disc playback** and choose **Open disc menu**.

For one-time use, press and hold the detail page's play button on Apple TV, or touch and hold it on iPhone/iPad. Choose the offered **Open disc menu** or **Play video** alternative.

Plozz supports HDMV (non-Java) menus. BD-J (Java-based) menus use video playback instead; recognizing a menu does not guarantee every disc will work. Disc folders stay in Plozz and cannot be downloaded.

## Navigate the menu

On Apple TV, use the remote's directions and center button to select disc options. While navigating the menu, press Back to open Plozz's **Disc menu** actions rather than close the player.

On iPhone/iPad, tap a disc option or use the onscreen direction and **Select** buttons. The optical-disc button opens Plozz's Disc menu actions.

Choose **Open disc menu** to return to the disc's menu, or **Close player** to leave.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Menu playback and videos chosen from **Disc videos** do not update your saved progress, watched status, or trackers. They do not use normal intro skipping or next-episode autoplay. Choose **Play video** for ordinary viewing and history.

## Choose a video or recover a menu

Open **Disc videos** from the player controls or Disc menu actions. Compare the numbered playlists by duration and chapter count, then select one; it starts at the beginning. Playlist numbers are disc identifiers, not episode names.

For **Disc menu unavailable**, choose **Retry**, **Try another region**, or **Play video** to bypass menus. **Disc videos** offers another route if the folder can be read. If that list fails, use Retry there.

Check missing or changed files through [Browse Files](/network-shares/#find-files-and-refresh), then rescan before retrying.
